I hold a Starting Research Position at Inria, in the [Atlantis](http://www-sop.inria.fr/atlantis/) project-team, where I work on **neural operators for Maxwell's equations**.

My main goal is to accelerate traditional numerical simulations using machine learning. I am interested in **neural operators**, **neural generative models** and **Markov chain Monte Carlo** methods applied to a broad range of physics problems (cosmology, fluid dynamics, electromagnetism).

Before Inria, I was a postdoctoral researcher at [CEA](https://www.cea.fr/), building fast surrogate models for blast-wave propagation with uncertainty quantification. I did my PhD at [Laboratoire de Mathématiques Blaise Pascal](https://lmbp.uca.fr/) (Université Clermont Auvergne) on non-reversible and generative sampling algorithms for cosmology, as a member of the [Aquila](https://www.aquila-consortium.org/) consortium.
